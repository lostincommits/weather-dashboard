# Weather Log

A few weather observations from old notes.

March:
The mornings were mostly clear. One entry mentions a particularly
pleasant morning, although the exact temperature was never recorded.

July:
Several days were humid. One note simply says:

"18 July — changed plans because of the weather."

I don't remember what the original plan was.

November:
The weather was quieter than expected. One entry was marked:

"24 November — nothing unusual."

These notes were written at different times, so the dates may not
represent the actual day the observations were made.
